      Idea Shard

      Idea Shard

      Through the eternal idea, manifesting the miracle of human agency across space.


      The Idea Shard is a quenched shard saturated with media, capable of storing an iota and a sequence of patterns. Right-click with an Idea Shard to execute its inscribed spell. If the stored iota is a vector or entity, that position is always considered within casting range — regardless of distance. The shard is consumed upon use. All media costs are drawn from the shard's own reserves — 30 amethyst dust.


      Inscribe Idea Shard (any, [pattern] →)

      • Renders
        • Palettes
          Your browser does not support visualizing patterns. Pattern code: waqqqq

          Accepts an iota and a list of patterns. Consumes one Quenched Shard from the offhand and produces an Idea Shard at the caster's position, with the target and spell inscribed within.

                          Soul Projection

                          Soul Projection (entity →)

                          • Renders
                            • Palettes
                              Your browser does not support visualizing patterns. Pattern code: wqqqqwqedwwde

                              By projecting a sliver of your soul into an entity within the same dimension, you may share that entity's vision. Only scrolls, allays, hex casters, and certain ethereal entities can serve as targets. Targeting yourself restores your normal sight. Consumes 5 amethyst dust. Curiously, when viewing through another caster's eyes, you cannot see your own body.
