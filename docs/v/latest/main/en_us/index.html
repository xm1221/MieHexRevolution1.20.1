      Patterns

      A list of all the patterns I've discovered, as well as what they do.

      Numerical Extremes

      Maximum Reflection (→ num)

      • Renders
        • Palettes
          Your browser does not support visualizing patterns. Pattern code: eeee

          I push against the boundaries of the world itself, and find the greatest number that can be expressed within this reality. Returns the largest possible numerical value.


          Minimum Reflection (→ num)

          • Renders
            • Palettes
              Your browser does not support visualizing patterns. Pattern code: qqqq

              I dive to the deepest depths of negation, finding the smallest number this world can contain. Returns the smallest possible numerical value (the negation of the maximum).


              Soul Projection

              Soul Projection (entity →)

              • Renders
                • Palettes
                  Your browser does not support visualizing patterns. Pattern code: wqqqqwqedwwde

                  By projecting a sliver of your soul into an entity within the same dimension, you may share that entity's vision. Only scrolls, allays, hex casters, and certain ethereal entities can serve as targets. Targeting yourself restores your normal sight. Consumes 5 amethyst dust. Curiously, when viewing through another caster's eyes, you cannot see your own body.


                  Spiritual Purification (player →)

                  • Renders
                    • Palettes
                      Your browser does not support visualizing patterns. Pattern code: qwawqwaqewdwewd

                      Obtains the entity into which the target player has projected their soul. If the target is viewing through their own body, the wrath of consciousness will be incurred.


                      Spells

                      Patterns and actions that perform a magical effect on the world.

                      Spellbook Spell

                      Scholar's Page-Turn (int →)

                      • Renders
                        • Palettes
                          Your browser does not support visualizing patterns. Pattern code: qqadad

                          By imbuing the spellbook in my offhand with a touch of will, I can flip through its pages without lifting a finger. Accepts a non-negative integer and rotates the spellbook's current page forward by that many pages. Requires a spellbook in the offhand.


                          Chunk Loading

                          Temporal Anchor (vector, int →)

                          • Renders
                            • Palettes
                              Your browser does not support visualizing patterns. Pattern code: aawewewaqweedeewqawewe

                              I anchor a region of space to the ticking of the world's heart, compelling it to remain loaded for a time. Accepts a position vector and a duration in ticks. The chunk containing the position will be kept loaded for the specified duration. Media cost scales with duration.


                              Remote Block Interaction

                              Void Hand (vector →)

                              • Renders
                                • Palettes
                                  Your browser does not support visualizing patterns. Pattern code: deewqqa

                                  I reach across the void and interact with a block as if I were standing before it. Accepts a position vector. Simulates a right-click on the block at that location. The block must be within normal casting range.
